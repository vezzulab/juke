**Linux**

- **An Advanced equalizer.** The amber *Advanced* button opens a bigger equalizer: a graph with its dB grid and a rack of one-tap controls for echo, reverb, clarity, shrill voices and tight bass, which tidy up a boomy or echoey room on top of your curve and your listening setting. *Basic* takes you back.
- **Listening settings** for where you listen — small room, small / medium / large speakers, headphones, 5.1 and 7.1 — that sit on top of your own curve.
- **Check for updates** now also has a button in *Settings ▸ General*, besides the one in the menu.
- Fixed: the music was too quiet with the equalizer on. An equalizer for each song or group, drop songs on an open list, and the computer stays awake while music plays (from the previous build).
